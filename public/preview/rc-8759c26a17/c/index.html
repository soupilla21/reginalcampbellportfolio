Questions

Good things to ask.

Who is the mentorship for?

Anyone who wants to build in Chicago real estate: first-time and early-stage investors, aspiring developers, contractors, brokers and other professionals.

What does it cost?

Tuition is $3,500, with a three-payment plan available. The Developer's Circle is a one-time $500 for three months.

Do I need money saved to start?

Not always. Many members start by building business credit or partnering on a first deal. On your call we'll talk through what's realistic for you.

Is funding guaranteed?

No. We help you prepare and introduce you to lending partners, and lenders make the final call.

What happens on the strategy call?

A real conversation about your goals. If the program isn't the right next step, we'll tell you.
