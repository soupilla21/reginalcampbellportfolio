Sheet A-01 / Origin

Raised here. Came back to build here.

Rukeem Campbell grew up in Chicago neighborhoods that could have limited him. Mentors showed him another path, and that path ran through an eight-year corporate career and years in real estate and construction.

He came back to build the network he wished he'd had: developers, investors, contractors, architects, engineers and brokers in one room. The mission is simple. Knowledge is the greatest equalizer.

Sheet A-06 / Specs

Common questions.

Q1Who is the mentorship for?

Aspiring and early-stage developers and investors, plus contractors, brokers and other professionals working in Chicago real estate.

Q2What does it cost?

Tuition is $3,500, with a three-payment plan available. The Developer's Circle is a one-time $500 for three months.

Q3Do I need capital to start?

Not always. Many members start by building business credit or partnering on a first deal. On your call we'll talk through what's realistic for you.

Q4Is funding guaranteed?

No. We help you prepare and connect you with lending partners, and lenders decide.

Q5What happens on the strategy call?

You'll walk through your goals and we'll tell you honestly whether the program is the right next step.
