06 — Questions

Before you call.

Who is the mentorship for?

Aspiring and early-stage developers and investors, plus contractors, brokers and other professionals who want to work on Chicago real estate. You don't need a deal already lined up.

What does it cost?

Tuition is $3,500, with a three-payment plan available. The Developer's Circle is a one-time $500 for three months of access.

Do I need money to start?

Not always. Many members start by building business credit or partnering on a first deal. On your call we'll talk through what's realistic for you.

Is funding guaranteed?

No. We help you prepare and connect you with lending partners, and lenders make the decisions.

What happens on the strategy call?

You'll talk through where you are and what you want to build, and we'll tell you honestly whether the program is the right next step.
