For a long time, this site was a single HTML page. That was fine for a homepage, but it meant every article I might publish would resolve to the same URL, get the same title, and produce the same social preview card — no matter what it was actually about.

This post is the first thing published on the new article platform: real routes, per-article metadata, and a feed. It exists to prove the pipeline end to end before anything substantive goes out under it.

## What changes

- Each article now gets its own URL, its own title and description, and its own social card.
- Search engines can index and rank each article independently, instead of every link pointing back at one homepage.
- Analytics can report on individual articles, including how far readers actually get into each one.

## What doesn't change

The homepage stays exactly as it was. This is additive infrastructure, not a redesign.

More substantive writing on enterprise transformation, AI governance, and delivery will follow on roughly a monthly cadence.
